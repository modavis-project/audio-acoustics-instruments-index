# Instrument design, hardware, and augmented instruments

Construction, electronics, interfaces, fabrication, controllers, and augmentation of musical instruments.

* [Azeirah/ocarina.js](GHIAO-0069.md) - Your ocarina is an input device
* [bb82dabn/laserharp](GHIAO-0024.md) - Browser laser harp and native VST3 instrument with mouse, keyboard, and MIDI string control.
* [bzelban/Theremin-AI](GHIAO-0023.md) - Recreation of an instrument with Computer Vision and Deep Learning
* [f-atwi/Stylophone](GHIAO-0019.md) - Proteus circuit design and implementation of a stylophone
* [hughpyle/directsynth](GHIAO-0034.md) - Additive synthesizer for Teensy
* [jacktipper/raivBox](GHIAO-0369.md) - Master's Thesis Project - NYU Steinhardt Music Technology - The raivBox is a standalone neural audio synthesis device based on Google Magenta's DDSP, with an NVIDIA Jetson Nano 2GB embedded development board at its core.
* [kimhinton/keystrum](GHIAO-0035.md) - Practice guitar chords without a guitar — in your browser. Strum 6 chords on your QWERTY keyboard. 4 rows = 4 strings, 6 columns = 6 chords. Karplus-Strong synthesis, strum detection, 3 folk songs. For beginners, DAW producers, teachers. MIT.
* [korginc/logue-sdk](GHIAO-0373.md) - This repository contains all the files and tools needed to build custom oscillators and effects for the prologue synthesizer.
* [MustafaLotfi/Paper-Piano](GHIAO-0029.md) - Using this program, you can play or control music by hand gesture. Also you can play a Paper Piano.
* [nicorobo/Harmidi](GHIAO-0028.md) - Harmidi is an in-browser MIDI controller that turns your computer keyboard into a multi-instrument workstation. Built for traveling musicians and harmony magicians, it offers a flexible and intuitive way to explore musical ideas while on the go or in the studio.
* [Nikelroid/dastan-gesture-guitar](GHIAO-0025.md) - 🎸 Play guitar with your bare hands — webcam gesture chords, Persian & flamenco rhythms, a theremin-style melody hand, and in-browser recording. One dependency-free HTML file.
* [pitchoilcan/Digidrumduino](GHIAO-0017.md) - An electronic steeldrum (Steelpan)
* [PlayNoise/PlayNoise.js](GHIAO-0027.md) - Creating music with the browser.  From Speech to Musical Instrument
* [probonopd/MiniDexed](GHIAO-0397.md) - Dexed FM synthesizer similar to 8x DX7 (TX816/TX802) running on a bare metal Raspberry Pi (without a Linux kernel or operating system)
* [Puara/GuitarAMI](GHIAO-0015.md) - The GuitarAMI is an Augmented Musical Instrument (AMI) using an acoustic guitar. The AMI is part of the Puara framework.
* [reppr/pulses](GHIAO-0018.md) - harmonical music instrument using ESP32
* [SabinT/musicbox-svg](GHIAO-0307.md) - Converts MIDI files to SVG in the format of music box paper tape
* [sandrolab/Coda-88](GHIAO-0021.md) - Minimal piano sound module with dynamics based on Arduino and WAV Trigger
* [sichengchen/theremin](GHIAO-0022.md) - A browser-based Theremin controlled with hand tracking.
* [SMCFY/fiddl](GHIAO-0033.md) - Sample based software instrument with real-time sound manipulation, utilizing touch gestures
* [sozbtn/physical-modelling-percussion-instrument](GHIAO-0031.md) - digital percussion instrument based on physical modelling synthesis, using sensel morph
* [striso/striso-control-firmware](GHIAO-0020.md) - Firmware for the Striso board
