# Environmental, building, and transport acoustics

Noise, sound transmission, acoustic comfort, and assessment in buildings, environments, or transport systems.

* [Code-TYMPAN/code_tympan](GHIAO-0255.md) - Umwelt-, Gebäude- oder Verkehrsakustik und Lärmkartierung.
* [dbetchkal/NMSIM-Python](GHIAO-0256.md) - A Python-based wrapper for Noise Model Simulation (NMSIM). Eases the creation of input files and joining data from GIS databases.
* [KennethKJ/SoundPropagationModeling](GHIAO-0246.md) - Software for modeling outdoor sound propagation, including the influence of the ground effect, atmospheric attenuation, and turbulence
* [pjazdzyk/sound-dampening-analyzer](GHIAO-0203.md) - Noise generation, propagation and dampening C# library for ventilation ducts based on VDI-2081 standard.
* [sparrow-acoustics/sparrowpy](GHIAO-0254.md) - Sound Propagation with Acoustic Radiosity for Realistic Outdoor Worlds
* [Universite-Gustave-Eiffel/NoiseCapture](GHIAO-0252.md) - Android App dedicated to the measurement of environmental noise.
* [Universite-Gustave-Eiffel/NoiseModelling](GHIAO-0251.md) - An open-source model for calculating noise maps.
* [Universite-Gustave-Eiffel/Rail4Earth](GHIAO-0253.md) - OnoSensor (Open Noise Sensor) empowering communities through complete noise monitoring — From acquisition to distributed networked storage and visualization.
