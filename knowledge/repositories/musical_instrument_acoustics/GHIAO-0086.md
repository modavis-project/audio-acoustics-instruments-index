# tonykoop/didgeridoo

## Summary

Stave-built didgeridoo design — 2013 acoustic study covering 25 musical keys (C1-C3, with build lengths from ~27" at C3 to ~107" at C1), frequency-to-length calculations, stave geometry across L:D aspect ratios 22:1 to 26:1, and design brainstorming for internal-rib patterns. Sister project to djembe and ashiko-drum-workshop.
