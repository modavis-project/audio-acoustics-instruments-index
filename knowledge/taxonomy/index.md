# Subject taxonomy

* [Aeroacoustics, underwater acoustics, and ultrasound](aero_underwater_and_ultrasound_acoustics.md) - Aeroacoustic, hydroacoustic, sonar, ultrasonic, or related wave-propagation research and tools.
* [Curated lists and meta-resources](meta_resources.md) - Indexes, catalogues, guides, and other curated resources that primarily point to relevant projects or knowledge.
* [Environmental, building, and transport acoustics](environmental_building_and_transport_acoustics.md) - Noise, sound transmission, acoustic comfort, and assessment in buildings, environments, or transport systems.
* [General acoustics and measurement](general_acoustics_and_measurement.md) - General acoustic analysis, instrumentation, calibration, measurement, and signal-characterization resources.
* [Instrument design, hardware, and augmented instruments](instrument_design_and_hardware.md) - Construction, electronics, interfaces, fabrication, controllers, and augmentation of musical instruments.
* [Instrument recognition, transcription, and datasets](instrument_recognition_and_datasets.md) - Instrument-centred datasets, recognition, transcription, alignment, classification, and learned models.
* [Musical instrument acoustics](musical_instrument_acoustics.md) - Research and simulation centred on sound generation, vibration, radiation, or transmission in musical instruments.
* [Organology and instrument knowledge](organology_and_instrument_knowledge.md) - Instrument classification, ontologies, collections, terminology, cultural knowledge, and structured instrument data.
* [Physical and computational acoustics](physical_and_computational_acoustics.md) - General wave physics, numerical solvers, boundary and finite-element methods, and computational acoustic models.
* [Psychoacoustics and sound quality](psychoacoustics_and_sound_quality.md) - Auditory perception, listening experiments, perceptual metrics, sound quality, and auditory modelling.
* [Room and virtual acoustics](room_and_virtual_acoustics.md) - Room simulation, room impulse responses, reverberation, acoustic environments, and virtual-acoustics systems.
* [Soundscape, bioacoustics, and ecoacoustics](soundscape_and_bioacoustics.md) - Environmental soundscapes, biological sounds, ecological acoustic monitoring, and related analysis.
* [Spatial audio and auralization](spatial_audio_and_auralization.md) - Binaural, ambisonic, multichannel, localization, spatial rendering, and auralization technologies.
* [Vibroacoustics and modal analysis](vibroacoustics_and_modal_analysis.md) - Coupled structural-acoustic vibration, experimental or numerical modal analysis, and related tooling.
* [Virtual instruments and sound synthesis](virtual_instruments_and_synthesis.md) - Playable software instruments, sound-synthesis systems, physical modelling, instrument emulation, and synthesis engines.
